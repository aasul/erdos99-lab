What are we exploring?

Erdős asked a question about the best possible pictures of many dots: must they eventually include a triangle with three sides exactly 1 unit long?

You can explore pictures here. Finding a nice picture is a starting point, not a proof that it is the best possible one. No new solution to the problem is claimed.
